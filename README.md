### Referral discount

Samsung’s U.S. referral page advertises an extra **5% off eligible products**. Referred customers receive a unique code by email after registration, and the discount is described as applying to the first order, up to $250. Samsung lists offer programs for students and educators, key workers, military, government, and some employees. ### Trade-in credits

A trade-in can reduce the amount due at purchase, but the quoted credit depends on the eligible model and its condition. Samsung’s trade-in terms say the device must meet eligibility requirements, including powering on and having a functioning display, and Samsung can adjust or reclaim the credit if the device is not received on time or fails inspection.

Compare the trade-in offer with what you could get selling the device elsewhere. The Samsung route is convenient because the estimated credit can appear at checkout, but the estimate is not unconditional cash in hand. Read the send-in window and inspection rules before relying on the displayed amount.

## How to apply a Samsung promo code

When you have a code that matches your product, the process is straightforward:

1. Add the eligible product to your cart and continue to checkout.
2. Find the field for a promotional code or credit.
3. Enter the code exactly as provided, without extra spaces.
4. Apply it and review the order total before submitting payment.

Samsung says promotional codes are entered during checkout and should be reflected on the final page. Some instant discounts, by contrast, appear automatically in the cart without a code. Look at the savings breakdown rather than assuming every offer needs manual entry.

Before paying, check the product, discount, shipping, taxes, and any financing terms. A code that applies to an accessory may not apply to the phone you intended to buy. If the product is part of a bundle, confirm the discount is attached to the right items.

## Why a Samsung promo code may not work

A rejected code is not necessarily broken. Samsung’s troubleshooting guidance recommends checking the following:

- **Product eligibility:** The code may only work on selected models or categories.
- **Offer dates:** The promotion may have expired or not started yet.
- **Typing errors:** Re-enter the code and remove any spaces after it.
- **Cart savings:** Some offers only become visible in the cart’s savings summary.
- **Conflicting promotions:** Two offers may not be stackable; Samsung says the best applicable promotion may be retained automatically.

If the code still fails, test it with only the qualifying item in your cart. That can help distinguish a product restriction from a conflict with another offer. Check the error message as well; it may identify the reason. Samsung also suggests clearing browser cookies and cache or trying another device if the code appears eligible but will not apply.

## What to check before placing your order

A quick final review can prevent most coupon-code surprises:

**Confirm the exact model.** Similar product names can have different eligibility. A deal for one storage size, finish, or model variant may not carry over to another.

**Read the offer terms.** Look for dates, purchase requirements, trade-in conditions, and whether the offer is a price reduction, credit, or gift with purchase.

**Compare the final total.** Use the amount shown at checkout after the eligible savings are applied. If the cart has multiple promotions, inspect the savings breakdown to see which ones actually stuck.

**Keep a copy of the offer details.** If the promotion requires a later claim, trade-in shipment, or separate redemption, note the deadline and instructions before checking out.
